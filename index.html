01  /  About me

Where engineering performance, reliability and split-second decisions meet.

individual excellence → collective performance.

Trackside motorsport engineer with 10+ years in motorsport engineering and 180+ international race and test events across six disciplines — Formula 1, Formula E, WRC, Rallycross, TCR and the Dakar Rally.

I have built my career operating with a strategic mindset, where engineering performance, reliability and split-second decision-making meet.

My trackside career includes three FIA championship wins: the 2018 FIA World Cup for Cross-Country Rallies Team Championship with X-raid, and back-to-back FIA World Rally Championship Manufacturers’ titles in 2019 and 2020 with Hyundai Motorsport.

I was also part of building the Cadillac Formula 1 Team from the ground up in 2025–26, before the team competed in its first race, and contributed to the development of two all-new race cars from concept to racing: the MINI John Cooper Works Buggy (2018) and Hyundai’s Rally1 hybrid WRC (2021).

Beyond the car, I’ve worked as a high-performance teams and executive coach, and strategic consultant, partnering with motorsport-driven teams, CEOs, CTOs and team leaders to strengthen business strategy, leadership, team performance and company culture in high-stakes environments.

03  /  Speaker

From the pit wall to the stage.

Alexia speaks about what high-stakes environments teach us about performance: how elite teams make decisions in seconds, how reliability is engineered rather than hoped for, and how individual excellence becomes collective results.

Drawing on 180+ events across six motorsport disciplines, three FIA championship titles and the build-up of a Formula 1 team from zero, her talks are candid, technical where it matters, and built for leaders who operate under pressure. She regularly works with corporate leadership teams, engineering and technology conferences, universities and sports organisations.

Speaking topics

  1. 01

    The Undercut: strategy under pressure

    How race teams make decisive calls with incomplete data and a running clock — and what that teaches leaders about timing, risk and conviction.

  2. 02

    Engineering reliability

    Reliability is not luck. Building systems, processes and habits that hold when it matters most, from the Dakar to a Grand Prix weekend.

  3. 03

    From individual excellence to collective performance

    What championship-winning teams do differently: roles, trust, communication and the culture that turns talent into titles.

  4. 04

    Building a team from zero

    Lessons from the start-up phase of a Formula 1 team: structure, priorities and people when nothing exists yet and the deadline is fixed.

  5. 05

    Women at the sharp end of motorsport

    A decade trackside as a woman in engineering — and why diversity is a performance lever, not a box to tick.

  6. 06

    Sustaining performance on the road

    180 events, six disciplines, constant travel: energy, focus and resilience when the season never stops.
