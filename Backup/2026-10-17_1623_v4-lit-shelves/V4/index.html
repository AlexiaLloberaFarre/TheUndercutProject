02 — About me

Where engineering performance, reliability and split-second decisions meet.

Trackside motorsport engineer with 10+ years in motorsport engineering and 180+ international race and test events across six disciplines: Formula 1, Formula E, WRC, Rallycross, TCR and the Dakar Rally.

I have built my career operating with a strategic mindset, where engineering performance, reliability and split-second decision-making meet.

My focus has always been the same: to create technical systems, teams and organizations that turn individual excellence into collective performance.

My trackside career includes three FIA championship wins: the 2018 FIA World Cup for Cross-Country Rallies Team Championship with X-raid, and back-to-back FIA World Rally Championship Manufacturers’ titles in 2019 and 2020 with Hyundai Motorsport.

I was also part of building the Cadillac Formula 1 Team from the ground up in 2025–26, before the team competed in its first race, and contributed to the development of two all-new race cars from concept to racing: the MINI John Cooper Works Buggy (2018) and Hyundai’s Rally1 hybrid WRC (2021).

Beyond the car, I’ve worked as a high-performance teams and executive coach, and strategic consultant, partnering with motorsport-driven teams, CEOs, CTOs and team leaders to strengthen business strategy, leadership, team performance and company culture in high-stakes environments.

        From the pit wall to the stage.
