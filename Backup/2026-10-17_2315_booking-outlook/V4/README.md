# V4 — Material Study edition

The fourth website for The Undercut Project: a warm, editorial design built
from the interior and motorsport mood board (walnut veneer, ambient red,
cream linen, pit-lane red, golden-hour pit wall). Open `V4/index.html`.

## Design

The red lit shelves from the board are the leitmotif: they fill the Home
backdrop, every section divider, a full-bleed statement ("Strategy is the
undercut."), the palmarès board, the Motorsport backdrop and the footer, with
animated glowing shelf edges and a slow motion-blur pan. Captions use the
board's white geometric type stacked with an indent ("Pit wall / in
contrast // 001").

- **Palette**: cream and linen pages, umber and walnut dark sections, ambient
  red for emphasis, amber and gold for light.
- **Type**, taken from the board: Cormorant Garamond (light serif display and
  italics), Outfit (the white geometric stacked captions), Jost (wide-tracked
  uppercase labels), Courier Prime (typewriter
  captions and data), Allerta Stencil (the pit-lane stripes) and Mrs Saint
  Delafield (the signature).
- **Details**: a vertical "material study" label on the left edge, viewfinder
  brackets that appear when you hover a photograph, typewriter captions with
  "// 001" plate numbers, a three-part statement line under the hero.

## Movement

- The hero photograph starts as a motion blur and settles into focus, then
  drifts slowly; a soft light sweep passes over it; red light streaks cross
  the dark sections from time to time.
- A warm ambient glow follows the cursor.
- Section titles and blocks reveal from a soft blur as you scroll.
- Red pit-lane stripes between every section carry the sector name in a
  slowly moving stencil marquee; a serif italic marquee lists the disciplines.
- **Motorsport** is a list of "lit shelves": hover a chapter and its photo
  floats next to the cursor while the shelf edge lights up in red; click to
  expand the chapter. On phones the photos are shown inline.
- Speaking topics expand in place; offers are selectable cards; the booking
  calendar and the contact form use the same engine as V1 to V3.
