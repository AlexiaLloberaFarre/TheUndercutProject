# V5 — Lights Out edition

The fifth website for The Undercut Project: the warm burnt-orange, amber and
espresso palette of the mood board, with race cars blurring past behind the
content and the five start lights as the opening. No glassware, no tables.
Open `V5/index.html`.

## Design

- **Palette**: espresso and night brown pages, burnt orange and amber for
  emphasis, a cream "menu card" for the mentoring section, a full burnt-orange
  panel for the speaker section, a trace of teal from the night-race picture.
- **Type**, taken from the menu card and the restaurant boards: Bodoni Moda
  (high-contrast Didone display and italics), Italianno (the script accents:
  "the undercut project", "about me", "save the date", the signature) and
  Manrope (wide-tracked uppercase labels and body text).
- **Photographs** of Alexia in arched frames, like a doorway into the paddock.

## Movement

- **Lights out** opening: five lights come on one by one, hold for a random
  moment, then go out, and the page launches with the cars snapping from blur
  into focus.
- **Cars racing behind the Home section**: the Bahrain sunset pan, widened to
  full bleed, drifts slowly, with a second blurred layer and a canvas of light
  streaks sweeping right-to-left that speed up when you scroll.
- The teal night-race blur sits behind Motorsport with parallax, and the
  sunset race behind Contact.
- Between sections, a row of five start lights lights up as you reach it and
  then goes out.
- Film grain, blur-to-sharp reveals that slide in like a car pulling out, a
  serif-italic marquee of the disciplines, the "grid" career list (P1 to P6)
  with a floating photo on hover, topics accordion, offer cards, the shared
  booking calendar and the contact form.
